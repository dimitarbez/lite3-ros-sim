\section{Contract summary}

Conversation 1.0 разликува \code{accepted}, \code{started}, \code{delta},
\code{retrying}, \code{offline\_fallback}, \code{completed},
\code{cancelled} и \code{error}. Emotion-state 1.1 содржи sequence, ROS stamp,
turn ID, source, backend, valence, arousal и category. Expression-action 1.0
содржи generation, occurrence ID, action и start/cancel kind. Hardware
transport 1.0 додава session и monotonic transport sequence, без да ја менува
внатрешната state 1.1 содржина.
